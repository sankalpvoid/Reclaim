Privacy Policy

Reclaim is designed to help people understand, reduce, or stop smoking while keeping control over their own data. This policy describes the current Reclaim v1 mobile app.

Effective September 30, 2026iPhone & Android

1. Information Reclaim may collect

Depending on the features a user chooses to use, Reclaim may process:

  • Account information such as email address and display name.
  • Quit date, smoking history, cigarette counts, reduction targets, and related progress data.
  • Craving logs, coping-tool feedback, and mood or check-in information entered by the user.
  • Savings goals and progress based on smoking-related estimates.
  • Learning progress, reminder preferences, and app settings.
  • Community posts, replies, reactions, reports, and blocking choices when community features are used.
  • Limited technical and product-usage events used to understand reliability and feature use.

Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not intentionally stored in Reclaim's application database.

2. How information is used

  • Provide the user's selected quit, reduction, or tracking experience.
  • Calculate progress, savings, trends, and other in-app summaries.
  • Surface personalized in-app suggestions based on data the user chooses to log.
  • Sync account-backed data across sessions.
  • Operate community features and moderation controls.
  • Send or schedule reminders the user enables.
  • Diagnose crashes, reliability issues, and product problems.
  • Protect the service against abuse and maintain account security.

3. Analytics and diagnostics

Reclaim's v1 product analytics use fixed event names and coarse, allowlisted properties. Reclaim does not intentionally include free-form health notes, community post text, passwords, or email addresses in product analytics events.

Technical diagnostics may contain device, operating-system, route, or error information needed to identify software problems. Sensitive route parameters are filtered where supported.

4. Service providers

Reclaim currently relies on third-party infrastructure to operate the app, including Supabase for authentication, database, and backend services, and Expo-related services for app delivery and technical observability. Those providers process information according to their own terms and security practices.

5. Community content

Information a user deliberately posts in Reclaim's community features may be visible to other users in the relevant community experience. Users should avoid including phone numbers, addresses, private identifiers, or other sensitive information in community posts. Reclaim provides reporting, blocking, and deletion controls intended to support safer use.

6. Notifications

Notifications are optional. Device notification permission is controlled by the operating system. Reclaim may store reminder preferences associated with an account and may schedule reminders locally on the user's device.

7. Data retention and account deletion

Account-backed Reclaim data is retained while an account remains active unless it is removed earlier through an available product control or is no longer needed for operation, security, or legal obligations.

Reclaim includes an in-app account deletion flow. When deletion is completed, Reclaim deletes the account and associated user-owned Reclaim data. Product analytics associated with the account, including linked anonymous installation analytics where identifiable by the deletion process, are also removed.

Some information may remain temporarily in infrastructure backups or security logs where deletion is not immediate, subject to provider retention practices and applicable law.

8. Security

Reclaim uses technical controls such as authenticated access, database row-level security, validation, and encrypted network connections where supported. No system can guarantee absolute security, and users should protect their account credentials and device access.

9. Health information and medical advice

Reclaim is a behavior-support and tracking product. It is not a medical provider, does not diagnose medical conditions, and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Health-related educational content and progress estimates are informational.

10. Children

Reclaim is not intended for children where use of a smoking-cessation product or processing of personal information would require parental consent or would otherwise be prohibited by law. Age requirements may be refined before public release based on distribution-region requirements.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Reclaim changes. The effective date at the top of the page will be updated when material revisions are published.

12. Contact

A dedicated Reclaim privacy/support email will be added before public App Store and Google Play submission.

The support address is intentionally not published yet. The rest of this page reflects the current Reclaim v1 product and can be used during pre-release testing.
